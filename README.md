# VRChat Legends Chat

**Chat with your VRChat Legends friends from your desktop.**

Friends, groups, voice and video calls, bots, reactions and more, in a fast app for Windows and Linux.

[**Download the latest version**](https://github.com/VRChat-Legends/vrchat-legends-chat/releases/latest)

## Download

Get the installer for your system from the [latest release](https://github.com/VRChat-Legends/vrchat-legends-chat/releases/latest):

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| **Windows** | `VRChat.Legends.Chat_x64-setup.exe` (installs for you, no admin needed) |
| **Linux** (Debian / Ubuntu) | the `.deb` file |
| **Linux** (Fedora / RHEL) | the `.rpm` file |
| **Linux** (any) | the `.AppImage` file: make it executable, then run it |

## Sign in

Open the app and sign in with your VRChat Legends account, the same way you do on the website: your password, or Discord, GitHub or Twitch.

## Features

- Your whole inbox: friends, group chats, and bots.
- Voice and video calls with screen sharing.
- Reactions, replies, forwarding, editing and voice messages.
- Link previews with quick buttons, and YouTube that plays right in the chat.
- New message pop-ups so you never miss one.
- It stays up to date on its own: when a new version is out, the app installs it and asks you to restart.

## Privacy and safety

Your messages are handled by VRChat Legends exactly as they are on the website. The app just gives them their own window. Updates are checked before they install, so only genuine VRChat Legends releases can ever be applied.

## Help

Something not working? Ask in the [VRChat Legends Discord](https://vrchatlegends.com) or on the website.

Google may not let you sign in from inside apps. If that happens, use your password or Discord, GitHub or Twitch instead.
